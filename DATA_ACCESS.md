# SLA-LENS: Data Access and Public Materials Policy

## Overview

SLA-LENS was developed using **40 annotated online L2 Spanish classroom and language-tutoring transcripts** (21,835 sentence-level rows). The designated model-adaptation partition comprises **35 transcripts (19,214 rows)**, and the held-out silver-evaluation partition comprises **five transcripts (2,621 rows)**. One of those five transcripts also has an **expert-reviewed gold reference (653 rows)**.

These transcripts and their annotations are participant-derived institutional research materials. **The underlying 40-transcript corpus, gold/silver reference annotations, and source recordings are not publicly distributed or licensed by this repository.**

## Publicly available repository materials

The repository provides annotation, evaluation, and deterministic-reporting software; methodological documentation; aggregate corpus statistics; and example files or reports where release has been authorized.

- **Original Python source code:** MIT License, as specified in [LICENSE](LICENSE).
- **Original documentation, project figure, and approved public example materials:** CC BY 4.0, within the scope and exclusions specified in [LICENSE-DATA.md](LICENSE-DATA.md).
- **Restricted classroom corpus and participant-derived material:** Not covered by either public license. Access is governed by institutional permissions and, where applicable, a separate data-use agreement.

**Public availability is not evidence of consent to redistribute participant speech.** Any authentic transcript excerpts, annotated rows, evidence indexes, screenshots, or generated reports must be explicitly cleared before public release or inclusion under CC BY 4.0. Materials not cleared for public redistribution should be omitted or replaced with synthetic examples. Third-party materials remain subject to their own terms.

## Research access requests

Requests for access to de-identified transcripts and/or annotations **may be considered on a case-by-case basis** for legitimate research, subject to approval by the institution(s) responsible for the data, participant-consent restrictions, applicable ethics requirements, and data-use terms. Access is discretionary and is not guaranteed.

To inquire, contact **olga.kellert@asu.edu** with:

1. Your name, institutional affiliation, and research role.
2. A brief research question and description of the proposed use.
3. Whether you plan to train models, redistribute derived materials, or publish transcript examples.

Additional documentation, ethics approval, or a signed data-use agreement may be required before any data are shared.

## Potential conditions of approved access

An approved agreement may restrict redistribution, re-identification, publication of identifying excerpts or metadata, secondary model training, release of derived weights or adapters, retention periods, and access by unapproved personnel. Exact terms will be determined by the responsible institution.
